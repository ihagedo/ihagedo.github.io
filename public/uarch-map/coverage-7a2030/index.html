L1 instruction cache · ours, inferred

Tree-PLRU L1I replacement

The L1I picks its victim by tree pseudo-LRU over its six ways, as three pairs, like the L1D.

Alternative. Random or round-robin replacement.

What would tell them apart. An eviction sequence built on tree-PLRU state evicts the predicted code line, as Prime+Retouch showed for the L1D.

Direction predictor · ours, conjecture

Path history rewind

Each branch in flight records the head of a circular buffer of taken-branch footprints; a mispredict rewinds the head to that mark and pushes the corrected outcome.

Alternative. Each branch saves full copies of both history registers, restored on a mispredict.

What would tell them apart. No timing probe tells them apart; the simulator can carry either.
